WHY NOTEWELL

Designed for focus. Engineered for speed.

Everything you need to write and connect ideas, without the bloat of web-wrapped cloud suites.

⚡️

Local-First SQLite Engine

Your notes are stored in a dedicated local SQLite database on your Mac. Instant search across thousands of notes, instantaneous launches, and zero sync spin-wheels.

✨

Live Markdown Conceal

Raw Markdown syntax markers fade away while you read, giving you the clarity of a book. The exact moment your cursor lands on a line, markers seamlessly reappear for precision editing.

🔗

Bidirectional [[Wikilinks]]

Connect thoughts intuitively using [[Note Title]]. Notewell tracks all incoming references and displays interactive backlink pills at the footer of each note.

🏷️

Organize with #Tags

Tag thoughts on the fly with #work, #ideas, or #project/alpha. Click any tag in the sidebar to filter your note collection instantly.

🔒

Per-Note AES-256 Encryption

Press ⌘L on any note to seal it behind military-grade AES-GCM 256-bit encryption with PBKDF2 key derivation. Your secret thoughts stay private even if your laptop is borrowed.

📂

Zero Lock-In Markdown Export

Export any note anytime as clean, standard Markdown with ⌘⇧E. Your writing remains your property forever, readable in any text editor on any device.

PRIVACY PROMISE

No account. No telemetry. No servers between you and your thoughts.

Most modern note applications want your email address, your phone number, a monthly subscription, and access to all your thoughts on their cloud servers.

Notewell takes the opposite path: an offline-first desktop tool written to respect your privacy. Everything stays on your disk inside ~/Library/Application Support/notewell/.
